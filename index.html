Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

Welcome to Fero Scan. Your privacy and local-first data security are our top priorities. This policy outlines how we handle information within our document scanning and PDF utility application.

The Short Version: Fero Scan uses a local-first architecture. Your documents, scans, and annotations remain strictly on your physical device. We utilize a secure User ID (UID) solely for authentication and subscription verification.

1. Information Collection & Authentication

We believe in data minimization.

  • Account & UID: Upon your first launch, we anchor your session using Firebase Authentication to assign a unique User ID (UID) for security and subscription state validation.
  • Scanned Documents & Files: All documents, PDF files, images, and edits are stored locally within your device's sandboxed storage directories using standard application paths. We do not upload or store your personal files on our cloud servers.
  • Usage & Tracking: We do not track personal behavior, analytics, or search queries entered within the application.

2. Permissions

To provide core document scanning and editing capabilities, Fero Scan requests specific device permissions:

  • Camera & Storage Access: Required by the document scanner engine to capture paper boundaries, perform edge-detection, and import/export local PDF files.
  • Network State & Connectivity: Used strictly to monitor offline/online connection states and quietly check remote feature flag configurations or synchronize subscription tokens.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted infrastructure providers such as Firebase (for baseline authentication session tracking) and secure local storage mechanisms. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third-party advertising or marketing networks.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security practices including hardware-encrypted local storage (`flutter_secure_storage`) for subscription flags and non-destructive safe-write mechanisms for file modifications to prevent data corruption.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
