Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Welcome to Fero Scan. Your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how information is handled within our document scanning and PDF utility application.

The Short Version: Fero Scan is a 100% free application that operates entirely locally on your device. We do not require any user accounts, logins, or registration. Your scanned documents and files stay strictly on your physical device.

1. Information Collection & Accounts

We believe in absolute data privacy:

  • No Registration or Accounts: Fero Scan does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
  • Documents & Scans: All documents, PDF files, scanned images, and edits are stored locally within your device's sandboxed storage directories. We do not upload, sync, or store your personal files on any external cloud servers.
  • Usage Tracking: We do not track personal behavior, analytics, or search queries entered within the application.

2. Permissions

To provide core document scanning and file utility features, Fero Scan requests specific device permissions:

  • Camera & Storage Access: Required by the document scanner engine to capture paper boundaries, perform edge-detection, and import/export local PDF files.
  • Network State: Used minimally or not at all, as the app functions fully offline.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or external analytics tools that monitor your activity or collect data from your device.

4. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, tracked, or transmitted to external servers, there is no risk of your personal data being compromised from our side. All data created or modified within the app remains entirely under your control on your physical device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
